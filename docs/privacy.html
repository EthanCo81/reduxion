Privacy Policy

Reduxion

Effective Date: February 27, 2026

Overview

Reduxion ("the App") is developed by Ethan Co. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect (or don't) when you use our app.

Data Collection

Reduxion does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. We do not collect:

• Names, email addresses, or contact information
• Location data
• Device identifiers or tracking data
• Usage analytics or behavioral data
• Photos, files, or any other personal content

Local Storage

Game data such as your scores, statistics, and preferences are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Third-Party Services

Reduxion does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. There are no ads in the app.

Internet Connection

Reduxion does not require an internet connection to function. No data is sent to or received from external servers during gameplay.

Children's Privacy

Reduxion does not knowingly collect any information from children. Since the app collects no data at all, it is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at reduxion.privacy@gmail.com.
